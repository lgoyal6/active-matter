Study design

What is being compared?

The simulator ran four requested densities, four seeds, and two force-law implementations. Each run used 500,000 steps. The slope is fit after a 25-minute transient and summarized across the four seeded runs.

Open evidence gap

Simulation is not a biological result.

The behavior-cloning model and its 10,000-trajectory asset remain unverified. Independent biological validation is also absent. These results support simulator analysis only.

Methods and limitations
